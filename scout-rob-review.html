The idea

Your North Star is that cities with police stations and fire houses call you first. Scout finds them first.

Nearly every city has these facilities, and no council wants to tell first responders their building can't be fixed. The opening usually shows up long before a formal solicitation — a bond that fails at the ballot box, a budget squeezed to the point of canceling projects, a chief on record that a station is falling apart.

Those are the moments where energy savings as the financing mechanism becomes the answer that doesn't touch the general fund or require a new tax. Scout is built to catch them as they happen.

It runs on a schedule across Ohio, Indiana, and Florida, checks bid portals, municipal .gov pages, and local government press, and applies a strict qualification and facts standard before anything reaches your board.

The point isn't volume. It's getting you into the conversation early — while there's still a relationship to build, not a bid to answer.

How it works

Four things, every run — and the discipline that protects your name.

01

Hunts

Police and fire facility needs, plus the budget triggers that make savings-financing the fit — failed bonds, capital plans, distress.

02

Verifies

Every find is date-stamped and sourced to primary records. Roles, never asserted contacts. It never invents a figure, link, or name.

03

Logs

Qualified opportunities land on the Leo Eff intake board in a FOR REVIEW group — tagged by service line, type, and signal strength.

04

Repeats

It runs again on schedule, dedupes against what's already there, and reports the strongest finds each pass. No double-logging.

Reading the board

Two tags tell you how to sort every row.

When you open the intake board, everything Scout finds sits in FOR REVIEW. Signal strength tells you how urgent it is; type tells you how to approach it. Between them, triage is quick.

Signal strength — how urgent

Hot

Open RFP with a live deadline. Clock is running.

Warm

Clear need or funding gap. Real, but no deadline forcing the pace.

Watch

Early, or older than six months. Re-check before acting.

Type — how to approach

Trigger

Get in early. A condition that creates the opening — before any bid exists. Highest value for your positioning.

RFP

Respond. An active solicitation — the opening already went public and you're one of several.

News-Proof

Use as ammunition. Social proof and content, not a deal to chase.
