02

Production standard — both subjects

read before rolling
1
Restate the question in the answer. Answers must stand alone. "The reason Cleveland invested twenty-one million…" — not "Because…". We only keep their voice, not ours.
2
No abbreviations on camera. Say "Energy Savings Performance Contract," "project manager," "heating and cooling" — not the acronyms. (Same note that tripped up the PM takes.)
3
Say the full company name once, cleanly. Confirm the exact legal name at the top of each sit-down — speech-to-text has been mangling it badly, so a clean on-camera read helps captions too.
4
One idea per answer. If they stack three thoughts, ask for the best one again on its own. Short, self-contained takes cut into everything downstream.
5
Guardrail the language. "Update or new construction," never "failing" or "deplorable" in Leopardo's own voice — the city can say that about its old buildings; we describe the fix, not the failure.
6
Figures: use only what's cleared. See the figures table below. If a number isn't green, don't put it in an answer we plan to publish.
