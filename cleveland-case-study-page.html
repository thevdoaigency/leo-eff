Project Overview

A comprehensive upgrade, funded by savings

  • Infrastructure: HVAC system replacements, building automation systems, and rooftop solar installations.
  • Exterior: façade repairs and tuckpointing, partial walls, roofs, steps, windows, storefronts, doors, and garage doors.
  • Interior: ceiling replacements, LED lighting upgrades, plumbing fixture replacements, and general refreshes throughout.
  • Electrical: upgrades to provide whole-building backup power.
  • Operational cost reductions and procurement solutions save the City of Cleveland $3,682,398 per year, guaranteed.
  • A total investment of $21,176,787 across all five police district facilities.
  • The work was delivered by local union trades — Cleveland's Building Trades Council, with the work done by Cleveland residents.

The commitment continues

Police stations first. The firehouses are next.

The public-safety investment Cleveland committed to isn't finished — it's continuing. The completed police districts set the standard; the fire stations are the next facilities in a plan the city already announced, on the same terms: guaranteed savings, local union labor, and systems built to last.

A continuation of a public commitment — not a new ask.
