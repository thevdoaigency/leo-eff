Why This Works

First responders are the one line item a council can't comfortably say no to.

Every other efficiency pitch competes with the road budget, the parks budget, the general fund. A failing firehouse doesn't. No elected official wants to be quoted denying the people who run toward danger a building where the air works and the heat runs. That's the whole strategy in one sentence — pick the category no one can refuse, prove it once in public, and turn the proof into a platform. Cleveland is the once. Rob is the platform.
