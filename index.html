Why This Works

First responders are the one line item a council can't comfortably say no to. Every other efficiency pitch competes with the road budget, the parks budget, the general fund. A failing firehouse doesn't. Pick the category no one can refuse, prove it once in public, turn the proof into a platform. Cleveland is the once. Rob is the platform.
